Just found an animal? First steps

🧺

Keep them safe

Use a box or carrier, and keep the animal warm and calm.

💧

Water, not milk

Offer a little water. Ask a vet before feeding young kittens or puppies.

🔍

Check for a microchip

Shelters and vets can scan for one, usually free.

📞

Call ahead

Intake rules and hours differ, and injured animals need a vet right away.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

What we collect

Account: if you sign up, your email address, display name and a password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed) and can't be read by us.

Location: only if you tap "Use my location" or let the app estimate it from your internet connection. It is used to find places near you and isn't saved to your account. If you type a city or ZIP code, it is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find it on the map.

Browser storage: essential data that keeps you signed in, and, only if you allow it in Cookie settings, your last search and your light or dark mode choice. Favorites and pet profiles you save stay in your own browser.

Who else is involved

Adoptable animals come from Petfinder. Nearby pet stores and vets come from OpenStreetMap contributors. Some organization logos are loaded from Google's icon service, which can see which logo was requested. Maps and directions open in Google Maps.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information or show ads.

Your choices
